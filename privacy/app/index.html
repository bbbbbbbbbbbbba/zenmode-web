This Privacy Policy describes how ZenStream ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the ZenStream mobile application.

Data Collection

ZenStream does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. The app runs entirely locally on your device and does not require account registration.

Local Data Storage

To provide trial and subscription features, ZenStream stores the following information locally on your device only:

  • App installation time and trial status
  • Subscription status cache (to reduce unnecessary API calls)
  • User preferences (theme, language, sound settings)

This data is stored only on your device using secure local storage mechanisms (AsyncStorage and iOS Keychain/Android Keystore). It is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

Subscriptions and In-App Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed entirely through Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not collect, process, or store any payment information, credit card details, or purchase history.

Your subscription data (such as subscription status and expiration date) is managed by Apple or Google. We only receive anonymous purchase receipts to verify your subscription status locally on your device.

For information about how Apple and Google handle your payment data, please refer to:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Analytics and Tracking

ZenStream does not use third-party analytics services, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies. We do not track your usage behavior or share any data with third parties.

Contact

If you contact us by email, we will use your message only to respond to your inquiry and will not use it for any other purpose.
